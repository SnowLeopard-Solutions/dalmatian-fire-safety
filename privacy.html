Privacy

This notice is from Raphael Whitmore, trading as Dalmatian Fire Safety.

If you send an enquiry

  • The contact form collects your name, email address, optional phone number, building type and message.
  • The form is handled by Formspree, which sends the details to me by email. Formspree keeps a record of submissions on its service.
  • I use your details to reply to your enquiry and follow it up. I do not share them with anyone else or sell them.
  • I keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them, and for my business records if we work together.

If you phone, email or message me

I use your number or address to reply, and keep the conversation for my records. This includes WhatsApp messages.

Visitor counts

This site uses GoatCounter to count visits. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data, so there is no cookie banner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details I hold about you. Email info@dalmatianfiresafety.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how your details have been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
