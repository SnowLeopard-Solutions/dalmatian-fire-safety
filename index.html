Ref. 02 — Who this is for

Residential landlords

Freeholders and building owners

Purpose-built blocks of flats and converted houses. If you own or manage the common parts, the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 puts the legal duty on you, whether you self-manage or use an agent.

HMO landlords

Houses in multiple occupation

Shared housing carries its own risk profile and, in many boroughs, licensing requirements that expect a current fire risk assessment, checked at licence renewal.

Managing agents

Acting on behalf of others

If you manage blocks or HMOs for landlords, a clear report sets out what is compliant and what needs action.

Small business owners

Shops, offices, and similar premises

Since October 2023, a written fire risk assessment is a legal requirement regardless of how many people you employ. A one-person shop is treated the same as a large office under the law.
