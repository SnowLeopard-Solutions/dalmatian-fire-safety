Fire risk assessment pricing: what affects the cost

Fire risk assessment pricing isn't a single fixed fee. It depends on what's being assessed. Here's what actually drives the cost, and typical ranges to work from.

What affects the price

Four things mainly determine the cost of an assessment:

  • Size of premises — more floor area and more common parts take longer to inspect.
  • Risk level — higher-risk buildings need closer inspection and more detailed reporting.
  • Location — travel time within London is factored in.
  • Complexity — building layout, number of storeys, and any history of previous fire safety issues.

Typical ranges

Premises Typical price
Small premises £300 – £350
Medium premises £350 – £750

For landlords or agents managing multiple sites, a discounted rate is available across a portfolio — get in touch for a quote.

For larger or higher-risk sites, an accurate quote is provided on request, or the assessment can be referred to a trusted partner where that's the better fit.

Getting an accurate quote

These ranges are a starting point. An accurate quote needs a few basic details about the building — type, size, and number of units or floors. Get in touch and a quote can usually be given quickly from that.

This article is for general information only and doesn't constitute a formal quote. Actual pricing is confirmed once the details of the building have been reviewed.
