Coverage — Enfield and North London

Areas covered

Dalmatian Fire Safety is based in Enfield and carries out fire risk assessments across North London and the nearby Epping Forest district. Distance from Enfield is one of the things that affects a quote, so properties close to home are the simplest to price.

Where assessments are carried out

  • Enfield: Enfield Town, Southgate, Palmers Green, Winchmore Hill, Edmonton, Ponders End, Enfield Lock, Brimsdown, Bush Hill Park, Cockfosters
  • Barnet: Chipping Barnet, New Barnet, Finchley, Whetstone, Mill Hill, Hendon, Colindale
  • Haringey: Tottenham, Wood Green, Muswell Hill, Crouch End, Hornsey, Harringay
  • Epping Forest district: Waltham Abbey, Loughton, Epping, Buckhurst Hill, Chigwell, Theydon Bois

If your property is outside these areas, get in touch. It may still be possible, and if not, a referral to someone suitable can be arranged.

Property types

Assessments cover the following, each with its own page:

  • HMOs and shared houses
  • Blocks of flats, for freeholders and managing agents
  • Shops, offices, restaurants and other commercial premises
  • Care homes and specialist settings

Pricing and what is included are set out on the services page.
